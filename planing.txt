Two things to watch:

1. **Load the font.** Lexend won't render unless you import it, and you only need the two weights you use:
   ```html
   <link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Lexend:wght@400;700&display=swap" rel="stylesheet">
   ```
2. **Put the config under `theme.extend`.** If `fontSize` sits directly under `theme`, it replaces Tailwind's defaults, so `text-lg`, `text-xs` and `text-6xl` stop existing. Under `extend`, yours get added instead. (If you're on Tailwind v4, this goes in CSS with `@theme` rather than `tailwind.config.js`.)
